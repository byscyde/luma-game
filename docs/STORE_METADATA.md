# Store metadata (draft, based only on implemented features)

**App name:** LUMA: Restore the Wild
**Subtitle (iOS, ≤30):** Restore an island, one puzzle at a time
**Short description (Play, ≤80):** Turn ancient stones, guide the water, and bring a quiet island back to life.

**Full description:**
A forgotten island has gone silent. Its springs have stopped, its trees are dry, and the
animals have left. You can bring it back.

Turn ancient stone channels to guide water from the spring to thirsty saplings. Every puzzle
you solve restores part of the island — trees grow, ponds fill, waterfalls return, and
wildlife comes home.

• 30 handcrafted puzzles across six areas of Verdant Isle
• Splitters, crossings, levers and gates introduced one gentle step at a time
• Watch your island transform after every solution
• Optional goals for puzzle lovers: finish in few moves, without spills, and bloom every flower
• Discover decorations and achievements
• Undo, reset and free hints — no timers, no lives, no pressure
• Play offline. No ads. No in-app purchases.

**Keywords (iOS, ≤100 chars):** puzzle,water,pipes,relaxing,island,restore,nature,logic,brain,calm
**Category:** Games → Puzzle
**What's new (0.1.0):** First release.
