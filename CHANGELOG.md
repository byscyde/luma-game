## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

First playable MVP (internal; not released to any store).

### Added
- Water-routing puzzles: rotating straights, elbows, splitters, crossings, fixed stones,
  levers with gates and inverted gates, optional flower beds.
- 30 hand-designed levels across six areas of Verdant Isle, each proven solvable.
- Optional objectives per level: finish within a move target, finish without spills,
  bloom the flower beds.
- Guided first five minutes: tap-to-turn pointer, first tree restoration, obstacle lesson,
  and the first island milestone; short one-time introductions for each new mechanic.
- Isometric island that visibly restores after each puzzle: trees, meadows, streams,
  ponds, waterfalls, a mill, and returning wildlife.
- Restoration essence to awaken new areas; eight decorations to discover and show on
  the island; eight achievements.
- Undo, reset and unlimited hints; puzzles resume exactly where you left them.
- Settings: music and sound volume, vibration, reduced motion, anonymous statistics
  opt-out, reset progress (with confirmation).
- Original generated soundtrack and sound effects.
